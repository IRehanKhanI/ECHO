# TERRA-X (AnalogWindow) — Product Requirements Document (`prd.md`)

1. **Executive Summary & Scientific Problem Statement**
   * **Product Name:** TERRA-X (*AnalogWindow — Objective-First Planetary Analog Discovery, Temporal Windowing & Virtual Hardware Testing Platform*)
   * **Hackathon Context:** [NASA Space Apps Challenge](https://www.nasa.gov/analog-missions/) — *Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars*.
   * **The Core Problem:**
     1. Existing analog catalogs ([Preston & Dartnell, 2014](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/244C9EB88B74D6905133DD2228DD3112/core-reader); [CAFE Catalogue, Preston et al., 2012](https://oro.open.ac.uk/33373); [Europlanet PFA](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs11214-017-0369-1)) are static, hand-curated descriptions or purely visual texture rankings ([Qaidam Basin Siamese Network](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.08584); [Sanganeria, 2026](https://nhsjs.com/2026/finding-earths-best-testbeds-for-moon-and-mars-rovers-a-quantitative-terrain-comparison/)).
     2. **No single Earth location can reproduce the Moon or Mars**, and an analog that is valid for one engineering test (e.g., rover slope mobility) can be invalid for another (e.g., thermal cold-soak or radar ice prospecting), as formalized conceptually by [Foucher et al. (2021)](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/109579/).
     3. **Static matching ignores seasons:** Lunar south polar illumination follows seasonal cycles with low solar elevations ($2^\circ\text{--}6^\circ$ per [NASA NESC](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230006101/downloads/PowerPoint%20Presentation.pdf) and [Diviner Seasonal Polar Products](https://www.diviner.ucla.edu/single-post/2020/07/20/new-diviner-seasonal-polar-data-products)), while terrestrial analog processes in places like the [McMurdo Dry Valleys](https://mcm.lternet.edu/node/4946) operate only during narrow seasonal intervals ([Antarctic Mars-Analog Soils Study](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10322585)).
     4. **Instrument degradation & corrosion transferability gap:** Hardware tested in terrestrial analogs experiences moisture/chloride corrosion, whereas lunar/Martian hardware experiences hard-vacuum outgassing, extreme $\Delta T$ thermal fatigue, or Martian perchlorate/abrasive dust wear.
   * **Product Vision:** Build a working scientific workspace where mission planners select a **Planetary Target** (Moon South Pole or Mars Jezero Crater) and a **Specific Test Objective**, and the system returns:
     1. **Ranked Earth Analog Sites** (including a **Novelty Filter** to surface lesser-known sites beyond Atacama, Devon Island, Antarctica, Hawaii, and Iceland).
     2. **Exact Date-Range Analog Windows** (`Timer / Month-by-Month Calendar`) showing *when* the Earth location matches target conditions.
     3. **Multi-Site Mission Portfolio** (a set-cover combination of Earth sites that collectively cover all mission subsystems).
     4. **Virtual Instrument Testing & Fast-Forward Degradation Simulator** (`0–10 Years`) showing which specific component or fastener corrodes or fails on Earth vs. Moon/Mars and why.
     5. **Reproducible Analog Fidelity Report** with exact physical numbers, percentage similarity per dimension, and explicit breakdown limits.

4. **Mathematical & Scientific Engine Specifications**
   * **4.1 Per-Dimension Fidelity Score ($S_{d}$)**
     Instead of a black-box AI score, every physical dimension $d \in \{\text{Thermal Range}, \text{Diurnal/Seasonal Ratio}, \text{Slope}, \text{Roughness}, \text{Solar Elevation}, \text{Ice/Aridity}, \text{Radar CPR}\}$ is scored transparently for Earth site $i$, month $m \in \{1..12\}$, against planetary reference site $p$:
     $$S_{d}(i, m, p) = 100 \times \exp\left( - \frac{|x_{i,m,d} - y_{p,d}|}{\sigma_d} \right)$$
     where $x_{i,m,d}$ is the Earth physical measurement in month $m$, $y_{p,d}$ is the target planetary measurement (or scaled dimensionless ratio for dynamics where absolute values cannot match on Earth, such as lunar $-235^\circ\text{C}$ crater shadows), and $\sigma_d$ is the domain tolerance scale for dimension $d$.
   * **4.2 Objective-Weighted Transferability Score ($F_{\text{obj}}$)**
     Each user-selected **Test Objective** $O$ defines a normalized weight vector $\mathbf{w}^{(O)} = (w_1, w_2, \dots, w_D)$ with $\sum_{d=1}^D w_d = 1$:
     $$F_{\text{obj}}(i, m, p, O) = \sum_{d=1}^{D} w_d^{(O)} \cdot S_d(i, m, p)$$
     * **Starter Objective Weight Profiles:**
       1. *Low-Sun-Angle Vision & Autonomous Navigation:* Solar Elevation ($w = 0.45$), Slope ($w = 0.25$), Surface Roughness/Shadow Contrast ($w = 0.20$), Albedo ($w = 0.10$), Thermal ($w = 0.00$).
       2. *Thermal Cycling & Hardware Cold-Soak:* Diurnal $\Delta T$ Amplitude ($w = 0.40$), Minimum Temperature Extremes ($w = 0.35$), Seasonal-to-Diurnal Ratio ($w = 0.15$), Aridity/Wind ($w = 0.10$).
       3. *Ice-Bearing Regolith Excavation & Drilling:* Permafrost/Ground-Ice Stability ($w = 0.40$), Subsurface Temperature ($w = 0.25$), Surface Blockiness/Roughness ($w = 0.20$), Radar CPR Signature ($w = 0.15$).
       4. *Rover Slope & Regolith Mobility:* Topographic Slope Distribution ($w = 0.45$), RMS Roughness ($w = 0.35$), Bare Rock/Sand Fraction ($w = 0.20$).
   * **4.3 Temporal Analog Window Detector (`Timer / Date-Range Engine`)**
     For any Earth candidate $i$ and threshold $\tau$ (default $\tau = 75\%$), the system interpolates the monthly climatology curve into a 365-day daily spline $\hat{F}_{\text{obj}}(i, t, p, O)$ and extracts contiguous date intervals $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$ where $\hat{F}_{\text{obj}}(i, t) \ge \tau$, outputting exact calendar windows (e.g., `Nov 18 – Feb 09 (84 days) · Peak Fidelity: 89.2% on Jan 04`).
   * **4.4 Mission Analog Portfolio Optimizer (Weighted Set-Cover)**
     Because a single Earth site cannot satisfy all mission objectives $\{O_1, O_2, \dots, O_K\}$, the **Portfolio Optimizer** selects a small subset of $k$ Earth locations $C \subseteq \mathcal{E}$ (typically $k = 2$ or $3$) that maximizes total multi-objective mission coverage:
     $$\max_{C \subseteq \mathcal{E}, |C| \le k} \sum_{r=1}^{K} \max_{i \in C, m \in \{1..12\}} F_{\text{obj}}(i, m, p, O_r)$$
   * **4.5 Virtual Instrument Testing & Fast-Forward Degradation Simulator (`0–10 Years`)**
     Simulates cumulative environmental stress over time $t \in [0, 10]\text{ years}$ across specific hardware sub-components (e.g., *Rover Wheel & Suspension Actuator*, *Rotary-Percussive Ice Drill*, *Optical Mast Seal & Titanium Fastener #4*):
     * **Cumulative Damage Index $D_c(t)$:**
       $$D_c(t) = \int_0^t \left( \alpha_c \cdot \dot{N}_{\text{thermal}}(\tau) \cdot (\Delta T(\tau))^m + \beta_c \cdot C_{\text{corrosive}}(\tau) \cdot \text{RH}(\tau) + \gamma_c \cdot A_{\text{abrasion}}(\tau) \right) d\tau$$
       * **Thermal Fatigue Term ($\alpha_c$):** Coffin-Manson low-cycle thermal expansion mismatch across fastener threads and seals driven by diurnal $\Delta T$.
       * **Chemical / Pitting Corrosion Term ($\beta_c$):** Driven by high humidity/salinity on Earth coastal/playa analogs vs. perchlorate/sulfate reactivity on Mars vs. zero aqueous corrosion (but cold-welding/outgassing) in lunar vacuum.
       * **Regolith Abrasion Term ($\gamma_c$):** Driven by unweathered agglutinitic lunar dust or Martian aeolian dust ingress into rotary joints.
     * **Fast-Forward Output:** At any scrubbed timestamp (e.g., `Year 5.0`), any component with $D_c(t) \ge 1.0$ turns **translucent red** in the 3D CAD viewer and displays the exact physical reason why it fails on Earth vs. on the Moon/Mars.

6. **Computational Efficiency & Precomputation Architecture**
   1. **Offline Ingestion, Instant Online Arithmetic:** Raw satellite rasters ([LRO Diviner](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm), [LOLA](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/), [Earthdata MODIS/SRTM](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/)) are processed once into a lightweight, static JSON/Parquet descriptor matrix (`earth_candidates_monthly.json` and `planetary_targets.json`, `< 2 MB` total).
   2. **Zero-Latency Weight Recalculation:** When a user moves an objective weight slider or scrubs the calendar timeline, the browser computes the weighted dot product across all candidate cells and 12 months in `< 5 ms` directly in TypeScript/WebWorker.
   3. **Normalized Query Caching:** Custom portfolio optimizations and route profiles are cached by normalized parameter hash (`target_id + objective_id + rounded_weights`).

7. **Validation & Scientific Honesty Requirements**
   * **Known-Site Back-Testing Benchmark:** Automated unit tests must verify that:
     * **McMurdo Dry Valleys (Antarctica)** and **Devon Island (Haughton Crater)** rank in the top tier for *Ice-Bearing Permafrost Excavation* and *Cold-Soak* during their respective polar/summer windows.
     * **Atacama Desert (Chile)** and **Qaidam Basin (Tibetan Plateau)** rank high for *Mars Arid Mineralogy & Rover Mobility* but score low for *Lunar South Pole 2°–6° Grazing Illumination*.
   * **Explicit Breakdown Disclosure:** Every screen and exported report must prominently list the four unbridgeable terrestrial gaps: **Gravity** ($9.81\text{ m/s}^2$ vs. $1.62\text{ m/s}^2$ Moon / $3.71\text{ m/s}^2$ Mars), **Atmosphere/Pressure** ($101.3\text{ kPa}$ vs. $3\times 10^{-15}\text{ bar}$ lunar vacuum / $6\text{ mbar}$ $\text{CO}_2$ Mars), **Radiation** (Earth magnetosphere vs. solar particle events/GCR), and **Regolith Mechanics** (water-weathered terrestrial grains vs. electrostatic, jagged impact glass agglutinates).
