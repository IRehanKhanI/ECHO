## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, elevated synthesis of natural sciences, modern cartography, and human-centric software. It bridges the gap between empirical precision and organic warmth—evoking the measured serenity of high-end field research tools, botanical taxonomy, and contemporary wellness platforms.

### Core Tenets
- **Natural Organic Precision:** Visual structures prioritize generous breathing room, gentle continuous curves, and layered tonal depths rather than aggressive grids or hard visual divisions.
- **Warm Scientific Neutrality:** Substrates abandon sterile clinical whites in favor of warm calcified stone, pressed cotton paper, and soft cashmere tones.
- **Empirical Clarity:** Data, observations, and interactive mechanisms are treated with quiet authority. Color indicates state, taxonomic classification, or vital thresholds, never gratuitous decoration.
- **Atmospheric Touch:** Tactility is communicated through soft, warm ambient drop-offs and subtle parchment-like borders that feel grounded, physical, and composed.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical forest moss, rich terracotta earth, and warm resinous amber set against soft calcified mineral layers. Blue and cyan are strictly excluded to preserve an organic, earth-first chromatic temperature.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#2A5A44` — Deep Forest Moss):** Primary buttons, dominant interactive states, key navigational elements, and primary brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#E06A3B` — Terracotta Coral):** High-priority callouts, critical biometric points, focus states, and warm active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E69A28` — Golden Amber):** Secondary metrics, warning states, observation highlights, and specimen flags.
- **Neutral (`#7A7670` — Warm Stone Gray):** Secondary metadata, outlines, muted text, and structural dividers.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Alabaster Cream) serves as the primary canvas backdrop.
- **Surface Level 1:** `#F5F1EB` (Pressed Cashmere) for soft inset containers, sidebars, and grouping trays.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Parchment) for floating cards, modal sheets, and active panels.
- **Surface Borders:** Semi-transparent warm stone (`rgba(42, 90, 68, 0.08)` or `rgba(122, 118, 112, 0.15)`) to eliminate sharp, sterile contrasts.

## Typography

The type system balances organic geometry and utilitarian legibility. 

- **Outfit** carries headlines and titles with its open, rhythmic geometric forms, avoiding mechanical stiffness while preserving crisp optical weight.
- **Inter** ensures effortless continuous readability across dense observations, research notes, and user settings.
- **JetBrains Mono** is reserved exclusively for coordinates, scientific measurements, telemetry, timestamp logs, and raw metrics, providing unmistakable precision against the soft typographic backdrop.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy rejects rigid, boxed enclosures in favor of breathable fluid zones. Content groups float organically on top of muted tonal fields.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column adaptive fluid layout with a maximum container boundary of `1440px`. Column gutters remain spacious at `1.5rem` (`24px`) with outer canvas margins of `2.5rem` (`40px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout utilizing `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) canvas margins.

### Flow & Grouping
Vertical rhythm is paced generously to ensure zero visual claustrophobia. Spacing between major functional regions relies on `space-xl` (`48px`) or `space-lg` (`32px`), allowing cards to feel self-contained without imposing thick separation rules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via gentle tonal layering paired with soft, warm ambient lighting. Sharp drop shadows and harsh black shadows are strictly prohibited.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Flat Ground):** Background base (`#FAF8F5`) with nested grouping bays (`#F5F1EB`). Zero shadow.
- **Layer 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with an ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 63, 53, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 63, 53, 0.02)` and a soft stone border `rgba(122, 118, 112, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover / Active Float):** Elevated components and interactive cards lift subtly: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 90, 68, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(74, 63, 53, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Modals, dropdown menus, and popovers: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(42, 40, 37, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(42, 40, 37, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces continuous curves, soft edges, and pill contours, completely eschewing hard right angles or aggressive sharp corners.

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for compact utility elements such as checkboxes, tags, and small tooltips.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`) for primary cards, metric tiles, and floating dashboards.
- **Pill Architecture (`rounded-full`):** Buttons, interactive filter chips, badges, and search bars utilize full circular caps to preserve an approachable, tactile handheld feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`rounded-full`) in `#2A5A44` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#3B7A5C` accompanied by subtle ambient elevation. Active state slightly scales (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Warm cream pill (`#F5F1EB`) with `#2A5A44` text and an ultra-subtle border `rgba(42, 90, 68, 0.12)`. Hover transitions to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Accent:** Terracotta pill (`#E06A3B`) with white text, used strictly for vital primary actions or high-priority conversions.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded capsules (`rounded-full`). Unselected state features `#FFFFFF` fill with `rgba(122, 118, 112, 0.2)` border and muted text.
- Active state transitions into a soft moss wash (`rgba(42, 90, 68, 0.1)`) with `#2A5A44` text and a solid `#2A5A44` 1px border.

### Cards
- Resting on `#FFFFFF` with `1rem` to `1.5rem` radius. Borders use soft stone tones (`rgba(122, 118, 112, 0.12)`).
- Cards rely on generous interior padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) and tonal nesting (e.g. placing a `#FAF8F5` badge or sub-panel inside a white card) rather than divider lines.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Search bars use `rounded-full` contours with an inset background of `#F5F1EB`. 
- Standard inputs use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) geometry. Focus state introduces a 1.5px ring of `#2A5A44` with a warm glow (`rgba(42, 90, 68, 0.12)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are circular; checkboxes use `rounded-md` (`6px`) soft corners.
- Selected state fills with `#2A5A44` carrying a crisp white indicator mark.

### Data Metric Displays & Telemetry Badges
- Floating modular pills combining an `Inter` descriptive label alongside a high-legibility `JetBrains Mono` value (e.g., `42°19'N • 840 PPM`).
- Backed by light cashmere fills with a slight amber or terracotta dot indicator denoting real-time status.
